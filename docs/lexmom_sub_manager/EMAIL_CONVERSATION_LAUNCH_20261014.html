Who should receive Launch emails?

Target: solo, small and mid-size law practices with 1–50 attorneys, in any country, where there is a sourced website or client-intake need. Firm size is measured in attorneys, not all employees.

  • Named recipients: a current law-firm owner, founder or partner responsible for the website or client acquisition. Use a sourced business email and confirmed firm association.
  • General firm inboxes: a published, verified info@, contact@, office@ or similar firm mailbox. Ask for the colleague responsible; do not invent a person’s name.
  • Strongest signals: an explicit, current statement about building/updating a website or improving inquiry intake; or a recent, dated new-practice announcement that makes a website conversation relevant.
  • Needs research: a suspected missing website, a broken page or a directory-only listing. Check the actual official website and firm identity before making a website-absence claim. A missing Google result is not proof.

Illustrative fit: a newly established solo practice whose founder publicly says the firm is preparing its first website. This example is a persona, not a collected lead.

Choose another route: use Manage if the supported need is matter operations. Use Launch + Manage only when evidence supports both needs and neither product is clearly preferred. Keep unrelated firms, unconfirmed roles, stale/unverified emails and suppressed contacts held.

Personalization and sequence strategy

Use the actual sourced name, firm and one relevant published fact. A new practice or website need establishes possible relevance, not purchase intent. If evidence is weak, ask a question. Do not claim “you have no website” or “we built this for you” without verified facts and a real reviewed preview.

One contact, one sequence, maximum five acquisition emails: introduction, follow-up 1, follow-up 2, follow-up 3 and final feedback. Proposed send days: 1, 3, 6, 10 and 14, in recipient-local weekday business hours. These dates are suggestions; no sending schedule is active.

No response: next planned step. Opens alone: record activity without changing the sequence. A credible product click: focus the remaining unsent messages without resetting the five-email count. Any real reply: pause for review. Interested reply/demo request: human demo handoff. Confirmed signup or purchase: stop acquisition. Opt-out, complaint or hard bounce: suppress. After final feedback, close the sequence without automatic recycling. Product-event integration and automatic branching remain deferred.

Sender after approved activation: hello@meetlexmom.com. Reply-To: support@lexmom.com. Office inbox: mail@lexmom.com. Each live message needs the approved postal address and its recipient-specific opt-out link. Drafts below contain placeholders.
