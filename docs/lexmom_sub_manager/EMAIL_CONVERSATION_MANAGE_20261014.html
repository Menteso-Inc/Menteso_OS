Who should receive Manage emails?

Target: active solo, small and mid-size law practices with 1–50 attorneys, in any country, that manage client matters, tasks, documents or financial workflows.

  • Named recipients: current owners, founders, partners, firm administrators or operations managers whose sourced role makes practice workflows relevant.
  • General firm inboxes: a published, verified info@, contact@, office@ or similar business mailbox. Ask for the person responsible for matter workflows.
  • Strongest signals: a current, sourced statement that the firm is seeking case/matter management, billing software, practice management or a workflow system.
  • Potential fit: a qualified legal practice with real matters may be relevant even when its current software is unknown. Treat this as a low-confidence hypothesis and ask a question; do not assert that it uses spreadsheets, outdated tools or manual processes.

Illustrative fit: a small firm whose managing partner says it is reviewing matter-management tools. A confirmed administrator responsible for that evaluation is also an appropriate contact. These are personas, not collected leads.

Choose another route: use Launch where the supported need is a website/intake project. Use Launch + Manage where both needs are supported and neither is clearly preferred. Keep unrelated firms, unconfirmed roles, stale/unverified addresses and suppressed contacts held.

Personalization and sequence strategy

Use a verified name, role, firm and one relevant source fact. Qualification is possible fit, not proven buying intent. Do not accuse the recipient of using an “old school” system without evidence. Ask which workflow they would evaluate and invite them to inspect a matter in a demo.

One contact, one sequence, maximum five acquisition emails. Proposed days: 1, 3, 6, 10 and 14, in recipient-local weekday business hours. No recipients or active schedule are configured by this document.

No response: next planned step. Opens alone: no escalation. Credible product click: focus remaining unsent messages without restarting or adding emails. A real reply pauses the sequence for review. Interested reply/demo request goes to a human; confirmed signup or purchase stops acquisition. Opt-outs, complaints and hard bounces suppress. End after final feedback without automatic recycling. Product-event stops and automatic branch scheduling still need integration.

Sender after approved activation: hello@meetlexmom.com. Reply-To: support@lexmom.com. Office inbox: mail@lexmom.com. Use the approved postal identity and a recipient-specific unsubscribe link before activation.
