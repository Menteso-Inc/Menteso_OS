LexMom Launch + Manage

Email templates, audience and routing • 14 October 2026 • Drafts paused

Who should receive Launch + Manage emails?

Target: sourced solo, small and mid-size law practices with 1–50 attorneys, in any country, where there is evidence of relevance to both a website/intake project and matter-workflow management, and no product preference is known.

  • Named recipients: a current owner, founder or partner able to evaluate both the firm’s web presence and its practice operations.
  • General firm inboxes: a published, verified firm mailbox that can direct the message to the responsible owner/partner. Do not invent a decision-maker or presume that every administrator controls both areas.
  • Strongest signals: a recently established firm with a dated launch statement and supported needs for both website/intake and matter management; or an existing firm explicitly reviewing both.
  • Selection rule: assess Launch and Manage separately. Choose the combined route only when both have supported relevance and neither clearly dominates. If Launch has a specific supported need and Manage only broad firm-type relevance, prefer Launch. Reverse that where the strongest evidence supports Manage.
  • Needs research: uncertain firm identity, no website/intake evidence, unclear role, unmatched inbox, stale/unverified email or ambiguous product relevance. A combined message does not repair missing evidence.

Illustrative fit: a new boutique firm whose founder announces a website/intake project and a practice-management evaluation. This is a persona, not a collected lead.

Being a law firm alone does not make someone a buyer for both products. An operations contact with only a matter-workflow remit belongs in Manage. A founder with only a verified website priority belongs in Launch. Keep suppressed, converted or unresolved contacts out of new acquisition sequences.

Routing after the introduction

One person gets one sequence and a maximum of five acquisition emails total. Do not enroll them in separate Launch and Manage sequences.

  • No response or only opens: continue the next combined draft on the proposed cadence.
  • Credible Launch CTA click: remaining unsent slots should focus on Launch.
  • Credible Manage CTA click: remaining unsent slots should focus on Manage.
  • Both links clicked: keep both available or ask which priority comes first; do not infer a preference from ambiguous or scanner clicks.
  • Explicit product preference in a real reply: pause automation, review the reply and use that preference when deciding the next step.
  • A branch changes the content of remaining slots; it does not restart the introduction, reset the count or add emails.
  • Any actual reply pauses automatic follow-ups. Confirmed demo requests go to a human; confirmed signup or purchase stops acquisition. Opt-outs, complaints and hard bounces suppress. Close after final feedback without automatic recycling.

This is the intended strategy for review. Automatic product-choice branching and demo/signup/payment integration remain deferred. All campaigns are paused.

Proposed send days: 1, 3, 6, 10 and 14 in recipient-local weekday business hours. No active schedule. Opens are weak evidence; filtered clicks show engagement; a confirmed positive reply/demo request can indicate a Hot lead. Research fit and engagement temperature are separate.

Sender after approved activation: hello@meetlexmom.com. Reply-To: support@lexmom.com. Office inbox: mail@lexmom.com. Use sourced names, one verified relevant fact, the approved postal identity and a recipient-specific unsubscribe link. No unsupported “old school tools,” missing-website or already-built-preview claims.

Research and review checklist

Store the firm identity, country, size evidence, official domain, contact role/inbox purpose, email affiliation/verification date and source facts for each product separately. Record why the combined route was selected and any stated preference. Missing evidence stays held. All three routes use the current template version 20261014-conversation-v3.1.

Review audience fit, truthful personalization, product claims, proposed timing and feedback language before activation. Authenticated SES and small batches help establish sender performance; Delivery events do not reveal Inbox or Spam placement. Use controlled seed observations, bounces, complaints, replies and confirmed conversions. No zero-Spam guarantee is possible from current tracking.
