## Proposed two-specialist workflow

1. **Prospect Research:** discover official websites → research firm/buyer/contact evidence → deduplicate → qualify → verify published email → save durable records. Partial, excluded and blocked records remain visible.
2. **Shared eligibility gate:** require the selected product fit, allowed recipient/country/channel basis, sourced contact association as required by campaign policy, recent acceptable verification, no suppression/opt-out, no active conflicting sequence and remaining campaign budget. Hold uncertain contacts for enrichment or review.
3. **Email Campaign:** generate grounded personalization from approved Lexmom facts and the lead's sources → validate content/offer/links → queue the approved sequence → send within recipient-local timing and provider limits → retain the provider receipt.
4. **Inbound and feedback:** ingest replies, delivery/bounce/complaint and opt-out signals. Stop sequences on replies, declines, unsubscribe, hard bounce, complaint or conversion. Positive interest is routed to the configured owner/demo process; no demo booking or price promise without the required approved facts/integration.
5. **Learning/reporting:** separate provider acceptance, delivery, replies, qualified replies, demo outcomes and verified signup/payment. Feed source/segment yield back into research without inventing interest or automatically changing approved campaign limits/claims.

Design review: duplicate sends after crashes and opt-out/reply races require transactional state, an action key, last-moment suppression checks and reconciliation of unknown provider outcomes before retry. Email validity alone does not grant sending permission.

## Proposed continuous operation

Use the existing isolated AWS Lexmom runtime with supervised Python workers and durable queue/state. Research runs in bounded scheduled batches when eligible lead inventory is low. The email worker consumes due eligible jobs during campaign send windows; reply/event ingestion remains available continuously. Idle polling must not invoke the LLM.

Persist campaign/profile versions, lead stages, contact/source evidence, suppression records, scheduled actions, provider receipts, retry state and daily/monthly budget reservations. Recover after process/server restart without replaying completed work. Retry only transient failures with bounded backoff; stop on auth/quota failures and preserve a structured reason.

The dashboard needs Run/Pause controls, research and campaign status, usable contacts and held reasons, sends/replies/bounces/opt-outs, spend attribution and last successful worker activity. Global Pause blocks new discovery/sends; disabling email must still allow reply/opt-out processing. No timer has been installed yet.

Design review: 24/7 availability does not require constant paid API work or emailing recipients overnight. Persistent scheduling, limits and recovery are prerequisites, not capabilities of the current on-demand worker.
