LexMom five-email campaign review

INTERNAL REVIEW ONLY — nothing is scheduled or enrolled. Audience: sourced law-firm owners, partners and operations contacts, plus relevant general firm inboxes. Launch: firms with a supported website need. Manage: firms with a supported case-workflow need. Both: evidence supports both needs and no product preference is known. Never assume an old workflow or a missing website without evidence. One contact gets one sequence, with at most five emails total: introduction, three follow-ups and final feedback. Proposed spacing: days 1, 3, 6, 10 and 14, during the recipient’s weekday business hours. These are proposed offsets, not an active sending schedule. Personalize with a sourced name, firm and one relevant fact. Follow-ups are one continuing conversation: recall the immediately preceding email, add a useful point and ask one clear question. Before dispatch, require confirmed provider acceptance of that prior step in the same product/version. Never claim the recipient opened, read or replied without evidence. Response strategy for later activation: no response → next planned step; opens alone → no escalation; a credible Launch/Manage link click → focus the remaining unsent steps on that product without restarting or adding emails; any real reply → pause automated follow-ups for review; interested reply/demo request → human demo handoff; signup → stop acquisition and hand off to onboarding; confirmed purchase → stop all acquisition; unsubscribe, complaint or hard bounce → suppress immediately; final feedback/no response → close this sequence without automatic recycling. Activation is deferred. Real signup/demo/payment hooks and automatic product-branch scheduling still need integration after the Lexmom product work. Current mailbox monitoring and suppression remain enabled; prospect sending remains disabled. Before activation: validate audience eligibility, postal identity, opt-out, fresh verification and sender limits. Deliverability: use authenticated SES, one recipient per message and small initial batches. Opens can be inflated by privacy proxies and clicks by security scanners. Delivery does not reveal Inbox versus Spam. Measure controlled seed placement, bounces, complaints, replies and confirmed conversions; never promise zero spam. Review requested: approve audience fit, truthful personalization, product claims, cadence and final-feedback language. Approval of this review does not activate campaigns.
